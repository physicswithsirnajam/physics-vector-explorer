Stress analysis of plastics

Qualitative color fringes illustrate the principle of photoelastic stress analysis; the image does not calculate stresses in pascals.

Transparent plastics can become optically birefringent under stress. Between polarizing filters, stress concentrations appear as changing coloured fringes, helping reveal regions of high stress.
