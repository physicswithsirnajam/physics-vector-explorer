## Independent mathematics and conceptual checks

- **Q3:** (3π/2)/(2π) = 3/4 revolution (270°); **Q57:** 1.5 × 2π = 3π rad.
- **Q27:** at fixed mass and radius, F = mv²/r, so doubling v quadruples required inward tyre–road friction. Friction may be inadequate; slipping is a conditional, not an inevitable, result.
- **Q28:** on a frictionless, steadily traversed banked road, vertical balance gives N cosθ = mg and the inward component is N sinθ; N > mg if θ > 0.
- **Q38:** T = 2π√(980/9.8) ≈ 62.8 s. **Q75** continues to test original NBF 1500 m value ≈77.7 s.
- **Q60:** t = s/(v₀/2) = 74.8/3.4 = 22.0 s.
- Original V39 keyed worked cases in both rotational and centripetal topics remain under the existing regression tests; changes do not claim every original distractor has been formally verified against an external examiner key.
