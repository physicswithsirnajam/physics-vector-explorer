Orbit: gravity and free fall

The satellite and everything inside it fall together around Earth. The display is schematic, not to scale.

Key idea: The satellite and astronaut share the same gravitational acceleration. Without a floor pushing up on the astronaut, their apparent weight is approximately zero while Earth's gravity is still substantial.

Design your rotating space station

An occupant standing at the rim requires an inward centripetal force, supplied by the floor. The floor's support feels like weight. The Textbook Ex. 4.4 preset recreates the 1500 m station from page 73.

Physics note: In the inertial description, the floor exerts an inward normal force on the occupant. In the rotating frame, the occupant experiences an outward apparent centrifugal effect. This is simulated gravity, not additional gravitational attraction.
