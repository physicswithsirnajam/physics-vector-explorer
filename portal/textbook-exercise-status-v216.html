Textbook Exercises & Assignments: What's Available?

Choose a unit to find its existing worked examples, assignments and teacher-prepared questions. Every original National Book Foundation (NBF) end-of-unit exercise category has its own status, so you can distinguish currently accessible practice from material still awaiting teacher checks.

Important academic distinction: A working practice link is not independent certification that the exercise matches the original textbook word-for-word or that its answer is an approved FBISE marking key. The original end-of-unit MCQs, short responses, comprehensive responses and numerical problems are marked Source check pending until their original wording, calculations, figures and teacher approvals are verified individually. Units 6 and 7 are not approved complete courses or graded assessments.
