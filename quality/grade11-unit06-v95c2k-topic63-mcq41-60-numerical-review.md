### Important SLO release decision: all 20 remain supporting practice

This batch is mathematically sound, but **a question about Q=V/t is not automatically a direct test of B-35**, which specifically asks for ideal-fluid terms, **or of B-36**, which demands applying continuity. We should keep these 20 as **supporting learning/practice**. To offer verified summative coverage, use individually approved questions genuinely applying steady/incompressible/inviscid descriptions for B-35 and continuity `A_1v_1=A_2v_2` in suitable conditions for B-36. Do **not** infer that no flow-rate question can ever appear in an FBISE exam; this is a precise **item-level mapping decision pending teacher review**.
