At the same temperature, ideal-gas molecules have the same average translational kinetic energy, regardless of molecular mass. Lighter molecules have greater RMS speed. This simulator does not model rotational or vibrational internal energies.

Energy and molecular speed

vrms = √(3RT/M)

What changes with heating? Temperature rises, average translational kinetic energy increases, and the particles move faster on average. At fixed volume, more frequent and stronger wall collisions are associated with increased pressure.

8 MCQs

Teacher-led investigation: Predict → Observe → Explain

  1. Start at 300 K, 24 L and 1 mol. Predict the pressure when volume becomes 12 L without changing T or n.
  2. Move to Boyle's law: compare pressure × volume at 12, 24 and 48 L.
  3. Switch to Charles's law: double T in kelvin under constant pressure, and describe volume change.
  4. Switch to Molecular Energy: compare helium and nitrogen at 300 K; explain equal mean translational K.E. but unequal RMS speeds.
