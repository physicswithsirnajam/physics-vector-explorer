## Frequent physics/marking issues to watch

- **Signed area:** When force crosses zero, negative subareas SUBTRACT. B2-B answer is +12 J, not an unsigned 20 J.
- **Work against resistance vs work by resistance:** Friction performs negative work while positive energy may be spent overcoming it.
- **Physical reachability:** B4-B and C9-B ask about destinations beyond the stopping point. A negative *hypothetical* kinetic energy must be identified as impossible; use the actual stop distance.
- **Conceptual SLO B-31:** Keep clear emphasis on path independence, closed-path work and dissipation, not accidental higher-level practical/PBA-only content.
- **OR fairness:** A and B always share the same verified summative SLO and available marks. Teacher grading accepts scientifically correct alternate methods, correct signs and sensible numerical rounding.
- **Working:** Students should show intermediate formula, substitution, signed work and final unit. Merely stating an answer does not earn all allocated steps.
