**Technical distinction that matters:** The work during arbitrary loading is `W=∫F(x)dx`. For an elastic, nonlinear loading curve, the final force and extension do not determine `W`; for a linearly elastic, Hooke-law element loaded from zero, `W=½Fx`. The textbook's Figure7.14 plots **extension vertically and weight horizontally**; use the physically consistent **force vertical, extension horizontal** arrangement when teaching `∫F dx`. A green keyed source review does not exempt a question from clear axes or proportional-range assumptions.
