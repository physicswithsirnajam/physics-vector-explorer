Part A — Five textbook-labelled numericals

Textbook numerical 2.1 · P-11-B-02

Angle from the cross-to-dot ratio

Question: The magnitude of the cross product is √3 times the dot product of two vectors. Find the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Start with the relationship
    |A⃗ × B⃗| = √3(A⃗ · B⃗)
  2. Substitute the definitions
    AB sin θ = √3 AB cos θ
  3. Cancel AB for non-zero vectors and rearrange
    tan θ = √3
  4. Find the required angle
    θ = tan⁻¹(√3) = 60°

Final answer: θ = 60°

Examiner’s tip: Because the dot product here is positive, the acute angle is consistent with the given relation.

Textbook numerical 2.2 · P-11-B-01

Force magnitude from its horizontal component

Question: A force acts 30° above the horizontal. Its horizontal component is 20 N. Determine the force magnitude.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Choose the component formula
    Fₓ = F cos θ
  2. Substitute the given values
    20 = F cos 30°
  3. Rearrange and calculate
    F = 20 / 0.866 ≈ 23.1 N

Final answer: F ≈ 23.1 N

Examiner’s tip: Do not confuse the 20 N component with the full force.

Textbook numerical 2.3 · P-11-B-02

Dot and cross products of two inclined vectors

Question: A force vector of 5.5 N makes 10° with the x-axis, and a position vector of 4.3 m makes 80° with the x-axis. Determine the dot product and magnitude of the cross product.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. First determine the angle between the vectors
    θ = 80° − 10° = 70°
  2. Multiply the magnitudes
    Fr = (5.5)(4.3) = 23.65 N·m
  3. Calculate the scalar product
    F⃗ · r⃗ = 23.65 cos 70° ≈ 8.1 N·m
  4. Calculate the vector-product magnitude
    |F⃗ × r⃗| = 23.65 sin 70° ≈ 22.2 N·m

Final answer: F⃗ · r⃗ ≈ 8.1 N·m; |F⃗ × r⃗| ≈ 22.2 N·m

Examiner’s tip: Source-QC note: the position-vector magnitude is 4.3 m, not 3 m. Use the angle between the two vectors, not either angle measured from x alone.

Textbook numerical 2.4 · P-11-B-02

Angle from known scalar and vector products

Question: Two vectors have dot product 6√3 and cross-product magnitude 6. Determine the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Write the two given relationships
    AB cos θ = 6√3; AB sin θ = 6
  2. Divide sine relationship by cosine relationship
    tan θ = 6/(6√3) = 1/√3
  3. Evaluate the angle
    θ = tan⁻¹(1/√3) = 30°

Final answer: θ = 30°

Examiner’s tip: The positive dot product fixes the angle on the acute side.

Textbook numerical 2.5 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a 200 N force into x and y components

Question: A force of 200 N makes 30° with the positive x-axis. Find its horizontal and vertical components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Horizontal component
    Fₓ = F cos θ = 200 cos 30° = 173.2 N
  2. Vertical component
    Fᵧ = F sin θ = 200 sin 30° = 100 N
  3. Check the signs
    Both components are positive for a vector 30° above +x.

Final answer: Fₓ = 173.2 N; Fᵧ = 100 N

Examiner’s tip: Use cosine for the adjacent/horizontal component and sine for the opposite/vertical component when θ is measured from +x.

Part B — Selected additional SLO applications

Additional SLO practice 6 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a 40 N force at 25°

Question: A force of 40 N acts at 25° above the positive x-axis. Find its rectangular components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Find x-component
    Fₓ = 40 cos 25° ≈ 36.3 N
  2. Find y-component
    Fᵧ = 40 sin 25° ≈ 16.9 N
  3. Check sign convention
    Both components are positive in the first quadrant.

Final answer: Fₓ ≈ 36.3 N; Fᵧ ≈ 16.9 N

Examiner’s tip: State the unit N for both components.

Additional SLO practice 8 · P-11-B-01

Recover a Quadrant-II vector’s magnitude and direction

Question: Given A⃗ = −6î + 8ĵ, find its magnitude and direction measured anticlockwise from +x.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use Pythagoras for the magnitude
    A = √[(-6)² + 8²] = 10
  2. Find the reference angle
    α = tan⁻¹(8/6) ≈ 53.1°
  3. Identify Quadrant II (negative x, positive y)
    θ = 180° − 53.1° = 126.9°

Final answer: |A⃗| = 10; θ ≈ 126.9°

Examiner’s tip: Do not give 53.1° as the final direction: quadrant signs matter.

Additional SLO practice 10 · P-11-B-01

Find force magnitude and its vertical component

Question: A force makes 40° with the horizontal and has a horizontal component of 30 N. Find its magnitude and vertical component.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Resolve horizontally
    30 = F cos 40°
  2. Calculate magnitude
    F = 30/cos 40° ≈ 39.2 N
  3. Resolve vertically
    Fᵧ = F sin 40° ≈ 25.2 N

Final answer: F ≈ 39.2 N; Fᵧ ≈ 25.2 N

Examiner’s tip: Find F first, or use Fᵧ = 30 tan 40° as a check.

Additional SLO practice 16 · P-11-B-02

Dot product from three-dimensional components

Question: A⃗ = 3î − 2ĵ + 4k̂ and B⃗ = î + 5ĵ − 2k̂. Find A⃗ · B⃗.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Multiply matching components and add
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z
  2. Substitute the components
    = 3(1) + (−2)(5) + 4(−2)
  3. Combine terms
    = 3 − 10 − 8 = −15

Final answer: A⃗ · B⃗ = −15

Examiner’s tip: A negative dot product means the angle between these non-zero vectors is obtuse.

Additional SLO practice 18 · P-11-B-02

Instantaneous power from force and velocity vectors

Question: F⃗ = 6î + 8ĵ N and v⃗ = 3î + 4ĵ m·s⁻¹. Find the power delivered.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Recognize the physical scalar product
    P = F⃗ · v⃗
  2. Multiply corresponding components
    P = 6(3) + 8(4)
  3. Add and write proper units
    P = 18 + 32 = 50 W

Final answer: P = 50 W

Examiner’s tip: Power is scalar; the dot product gives watts.

Additional SLO practice 22 · P-11-B-02

Cross product in three dimensions

Question: A⃗ = 2î − ĵ + 3k̂ and B⃗ = î + 4ĵ − 2k̂. Find A⃗ × B⃗ and its magnitude.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Write the Cartesian cross-product expressions
    A⃗ × B⃗ = (AᵧB_z−A_zBᵧ)î + (A_zBₓ−AₓB_z)ĵ + (AₓBᵧ−AᵧBₓ)k̂
  2. Evaluate the î-component
    (−1)(−2) − (3)(4) = −10
  3. Evaluate the ĵ-component
    (3)(1) − (2)(−2) = 7
  4. Evaluate the k̂-component
    (2)(4) − (−1)(1) = 9
  5. Find the magnitude
    √[(-10)² + 7² + 9²] = √230 ≈ 15.2

Final answer: A⃗ × B⃗ = −10î + 7ĵ + 9k̂; magnitude ≈ 15.2

Examiner’s tip: When expanding determinants, remember the sign on the ĵ term.

Additional SLO practice 25 · P-11-B-02

Obtuse angle from negative dot product

Question: Two non-zero vectors have A⃗ · B⃗ = −8 and |A⃗ × B⃗| = 8√3. Find the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the magnitudes of the products to calculate a reference angle
    tan α = |A⃗ × B⃗| / |A⃗ · B⃗| = √3
  2. Evaluate the reference angle
    α = 60°
  3. Account for the negative dot product
    cos θ < 0, so θ is obtuse: θ = 180° − 60°

Final answer: θ = 120°

Examiner’s tip: A frequent error is 60°. The negative dot product makes the angle obtuse.

Additional SLO practice 30 · P-11-B-02

Integrated angle and product-of-magnitudes challenge

Question: For two non-zero vectors, A⃗ · B⃗ = 18 and |A⃗ × B⃗| = 24. Find their angle and AB.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Calculate the angle
    tan θ = 24/18 = 4/3; θ ≈ 53.1°
  2. Use the dot/cross identity
    (AB)² = (A⃗ · B⃗)² + |A⃗ × B⃗|²
  3. Substitute the values
    (AB)² = 18² + 24² = 900
  4. Take the positive square root
    AB = 30

Final answer: θ ≈ 53.1°; AB = 30

Examiner’s tip: AB is the product of magnitudes, not the dot product.
