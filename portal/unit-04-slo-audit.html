FRAMEWORK CLASSIFICATION MATTERS

2. SLOs we must not incorrectly insert into Unit 4

  • P-11-B-11 — Formative: conservation of linear momentum and simple collisions. The supplied framework explicitly marks this as Formative, not summative written-theory examination content.
  • P-11-B-12 — Summative, but different topic: elastic collisions, momentum and kinetic energy. It is a linear-momentum/collision SLO, not part of the Unit 4 rotational-motion chapter.
  • P-11-B-13 — Summative, but different topic: momentum in a closed system and possible energy loss. It likewise belongs to the earlier collision/momentum learning, not Unit 4.

Therefore the Unit 4 summative coverage set is B-07 through B-10, then B-14 through B-21. Numerically adjacent framework codes are not automatically Unit 4 content. The mapping follows both the book's Unit 4 opening SLOs and the framework classifications.
