FBISE source and assessment boundaries

Primary source: NBF Grade XI Physics, Unit 4 — Rotational and Circular Motion, printed p.53 chapter opening; pp.54–59 rotational kinematics; pp.59–64 centripetal acceleration, force, and applications; §§4.3–4.5, pp.64–71, moment of inertia, angular momentum and torque (including Example 4.3 p.70); §§4.6–4.7, pp.71–73, satellite weightlessness and artificial gravity (plus Assignment 4.4 on p.74); and centrifugation on p.63, Figure 4.11, with textbook exercises pp.74–76. Official assessment classifications follow the supplied FBISE HSSC-I Physics Assessment Framework.

  • P-11-B-07 — Summative (covered): Express angles in radians.
  • P-11-B-08 — Summative (covered): Calculate angular displacement, angular velocity, angular acceleration and related linear/angular quantities.
  • P-11-B-09 — Summative (covered): Apply equations of rotational motion to problems.
  • P-11-B-10 — Summative (Part 2 covered): Qualitative analysis of curved motion due to a perpendicular, inward force.
  • P-11-B-14 — Summative (Part 2 covered): Calculate centripetal force using F = mv²/r = mrω².
  • P-11-B-15 — Summative (Part 2 covered): Analyse inward force supplied by tension, friction, gravitation and normal reaction, including a banked road.
  • P-11-B-16 — Summative (Part 3 covered): Explain weightlessness and near-zero apparent support force for occupants of orbiting spacecraft.
  • P-11-B-17 — Summative (Part 3 covered): Explain and calculate artificial-gravity effects in an ideal rotating station.
  • P-11-B-18 — Summative (Part 4 covered): Moment of inertia about a defined axis and angular momentum.
  • P-11-B-19 — Summative (Part 4 covered): Relation between net external torque, moment of inertia and angular acceleration.
  • P-11-B-20 — Summative (Part 4 covered): Conservation of angular momentum and source-based applications including ice skaters and gyroscopes.
  • P-11-B-21 — Summative (Part 5 teaching covered): Justify centrifugation using centripetal force and material separation. Formal MCQ/descriptive item review is still pending.
