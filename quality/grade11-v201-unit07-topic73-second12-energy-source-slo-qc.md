### Original Figure 7.14 caution from printed page 122

The textbook's own force–extension illustration places **weight/force horizontally** and **extension vertically**, while the general formula `W=∫F dx` is the area under the **force-vertical versus extension-horizontal** graph. On a nonlinear curve, `∫x dF` from reversed axes is not generally the work. The textbook's zero-start **linear Hookean triangle** still gives the correct numeric `½Fx`. Q34 and Q57 require explicit labeling if displayed with any diagram. Q57's ordered coordinate pairs themselves contain the correct `(extension, force)` unit convention.
