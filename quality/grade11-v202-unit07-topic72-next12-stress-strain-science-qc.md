## Critical source nuance — Figure 7.12 and original book terminology

Printed NBF p120 Figure **7.12** plots **stress vertically** and **strain horizontally**. Its **OA** is straight and proportional, while **AB** has passed the proportional limit but is not yet past the elastic-limit point **B**, as explained on printed p121. Thus **elastic is not identical to strictly linear**, and a learner must not confuse the *proportional limit A* with the *elastic limit B*. Questions **Q30 and Q79** independently ask almost the same structural interpretation and refer to an external textbook graph; an approved accessible figure, equivalent standalone wording, and duplicate-selection review are compulsory before any standalone scored version. Do not silently attach copyrighted publisher page images to the public portal.

The textbook's source-specific **shear strain** relationship `tan θ≈θ≈Δx/y` assumes small angular distortion and radians. A conceptual recall item testing it can remain valuable as **supporting practice**, while a direct B-24 Application assessment should require real proportional reasoning or input-based calculation.
