Source and assessment boundaries

Our source basis is the NBF Grade XI Physics textbook, Unit 3 Translatory Motion, beginning on printed page 32, together with the supplied current FBISE Grade XI Physics Assessment Framework.

  • P-11-B-03 — Formative: Derivations of uniform-acceleration motion equations. Teach fully, but do not count as a final-exam summative SLO.
  • P-11-B-04 — Summative: Numerical problems in uniformly accelerated straight-line motion, ideal free fall and resolved horizontal/vertical components.
  • P-11-B-05 — Summative: Projectile motion in the absence of air resistance, including range, maximum height and time of flight.
  • P-11-B-06 — Summative, Part 2: Qualitative effects of air resistance on a projectile.
  • P-11-B-11 — Formative, Part 2: Conservation of momentum applied to collisions and related interactions.
  • P-11-B-12 — Summative, Part 2: Predict and analyse elastic collisions.
  • P-11-B-13 — Summative, Part 2: Explain momentum conservation alongside kinetic-energy changes.

Both source-based teaching parts are now available, covering pp. 33–49. A complete Unit 3 summative MCQ bank, full assessment mapping and any enrolled-only protected lessons remain unpublished. The special stick-together collision model is marked as an additional instructional case.
