# V45 — Grade XI Unit 4 Timed Projector Classroom Guide

**Physics with Sir Najam · Rotational and Circular Motion · PR #35**

## Purpose and scope

Teacher-guided, projector-friendly **80-MCQ Unit 4-only practice** built from the existing provisional `question-bank/grade-11/unit-04-v39-80-practice.json`, without modifying it or releasing any solution on the projected slide.

- **56 independent MCQs and 24 situation-based MCQs**: six full four-question situations, original stimulus shown on *each* of the associated slides.
- Correct options and explanations exist in the separate **public teacher resource** only; the presentation shows **question + A/B/C/D options**, never a highlighted correct answer, explained solution, student's choice or grade.
- Default **50 seconds per question**, with 40, 60, 75, 90 seconds and an **adaptive 45–90 seconds** option based on passage/question/option lengths. Adaptive is a display-pacing heuristic, not a learner ability diagnosis or an FBISE time rule.
- **Automatic advance is on by default**. Uncheck to keep the current question visible when time expires.
- Prominent **Start**, Previous, Next/Finish, Pause/Resume, Reset current timer, Fullscreen and **Go to Q 1–80** controls.
- Teacher keyboard shortcuts outside text/edit controls: right/left arrow for navigation, Space for pause/resume, F for fullscreen. If the tab is hidden or changed, active countdown **pauses** to prevent accidental silent skipping.
- Source-only public practice. **No authenticated exam session, grading, student activity logging, answer-key reveal or stored results**.

## Preparing a live lesson

1. Open Unit 4 Learning Desk → **Launch Unit 4 Timed Projector**, or Integrated Assessment Pack → **6. Timed projector presentation**.
2. Give students a **printed blank OMR-style Unit 4 practice sheet** from the Integrated Assessment Pack, before projecting any questions. The sheet is a manual practice aid, not a scanner-approved OMR.
3. Choose question duration and automatic advance behavior **before** clicking Start. The default is 50 seconds and auto-advance. For longer situations or calculations, use 75/90 seconds or adaptive.
4. Show the browser on your classroom projector. Use **Fullscreen** from a normal browser tab for the largest display. Embedded Google Sites frames may restrict fullscreen; open the portal page itself in a new tab if necessary.
5. Students respond to **one A/B/C/D choice** per question on paper. **Pause** to explain instructions or change pace; Next/Previous and Jump permit teacher intervention.
6. For the six scenario sets (Q57–Q80), the **entire scenario passage** appears along with every question; nothing needs to be recalled from a prior slide.
7. At Q80, select **Finish presentation** (or let it finish automatically). Correct options are not displayed. Open the separate teacher key **only after the session**, avoiding revealing answers while projecting.
8. Do not treat this provisional source-grounded Unit 4 exercise as a full-syllabus official FBISE paper or a protected graded exam. Teacher academic approval and actual classroom projection checks remain necessary.
