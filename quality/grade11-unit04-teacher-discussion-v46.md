# V46 — Grade XI Unit 4 Teacher MCQ Solution & Discussion Slides

**Physics with Sir Najam | Rotational and Circular Motion | PR #36**

**Status: provisional public post-practice teaching resource. Not a protected teacher-only page, board examiner-certified marking key or graded assessment.**

## Why V46 is separate

V45 serves a student-facing **timed, question-only** projector presentation. V46 is a new, separate, **untimed** public page to use only **after** students complete their MCQ practice. It draws from the same existing, provisional `question-bank/grade-11/unit-04-v39-80-practice.json` (80 MCQs: 56 independent and 24 in six 4-question situations) and never modifies the question bank.

The interface deliberately has **160 sequential teaching stages**: Question 1, explicitly reveal Worked Solution 1; Question 2, explicitly reveal Worked Solution 2; and so on through Question 80 and Worked Solution 80. Answers do not appear from the **Next** button or keyboard right arrow unless the teacher has deliberately pressed **Reveal worked solution**.

## What's actually on each solution slide

- Full original question stem and A/B/C/D option text, with the original keyed correct option highlighted.
- The complete **existing bank explanation**, which may contain equations and numeric substitutions; it is displayed verbatim as the provisional reasoning already present in the original bank. **V46 does not invent new extended step-by-step derivations.** Teacher review may identify items needing a further expanded solution before classroom approval.
- The source-derived **summative FBISE SLO**, one of P-11-B-07–B-10 or B-14–B-21, and the question's **Knowledge / Understanding / Application** cognitive level.
- Original question **topic** and NBF Grade XI Physics textbook **printed page**, plus a generic teaching discussion prompt suited to the cognitive level. These prompts are facilitation suggestions, not additional physics evidence.
- For each scenario item Q57–Q80, the original complete shared passage remains visible on both question and solution stages.

## Teacher classroom workflow

1. Use **Unit 4 Learning Desk → Timed 80-MCQ Projector Presentation** for the initial student practice. Students mark the separately printed, blank manual OMR-style response sheet.
2. **End the assessment**, collect or close students' answer sheets as appropriate, and then open **Unit 4 Learning Desk → Teacher MCQ Worked Solutions & Discussion Slides**, or Assessment Pack card 7.
3. Confirm the page says **Ready**, then select **Start discussion**. Every question starts **unmarked and untimed**.
4. Ask students to explain their choice, then select **Reveal worked solution**. The correct option is highlighted, the original bank explanation is shown, and the SLO / cognitive level / topic / textbook reference appear. Discuss typical distractors and numerical steps together.
5. Click **Next question** to move to the next unmarked question. **Previous question**, **Return to question**, or **Go to Q 1–80** always hide the solution for the new question; no unintentional reveal from a keyboard arrow.
6. Use **Fullscreen** in a regular browser tab for projector use. It may be unavailable within a restricted Google Sites iframe; open the presentation link in a new tab.
7. At Q80, review the final solution; the presentation is complete. No scores or grades are recorded, nor is any answer submitted anywhere.

**Important access boundary:** This is a public resource. Anyone with its URL can open it and reveal answers; the reveal button is a presentation safeguard, **not an access-control system**. Never present it as a secured, unpublished examiner key. Keep it off student screens before and during practice.
