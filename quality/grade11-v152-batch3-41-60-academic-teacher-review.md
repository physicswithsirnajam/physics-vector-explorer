2. **Shared lattice positions versus effective cell contents — compulsory teaching clarification (Q41, Q43, Q45, Q56 and Q57).** Figure 7.5(b) shows eight geometrical corner positions, six geometrical face positions in FCC, and one body-centre position in BCC. These are drawn sites, **not independent atom totals**. Contributions for a conventional unit cell: each corner belongs to 8 cells, each face to 2. Therefore the textbook's **net effective counts** are SC = 1, BCC = 2, FCC = 4 atoms per conventional cell. This guards against misreading the source's awkward wording, “eight atoms at each corner,” as eight atoms literally occupying every corner. **None of the five source MCQ stems explicitly asks for net atoms**, so the scientifically valid position-count questions may be kept unchanged; the teacher must explain the distinction.
