How to verify each held question

  1. Open your actual NBF Grade XI textbook on the printed page shown below (90, 92, 94 or 95). Do not treat our V90 study drawing as a substitute for the original illustration.
  2. Compare the printed figure's labels, arrows, orientation and graph stages against the original MCQ wording and answer options.
  3. Expand the teacher-study answer key and independently decide if it is scientifically supported. Source answer keys are not automatically official FBISE keys.
  4. Record your observations. Select Candidate for teacher signoff only when all three checks are marked and the notes are specific enough for review. Otherwise keep the question held.
  5. Copy the local review record and paste it back into our chat. Nothing is saved automatically, and closing this page discards the entries.
