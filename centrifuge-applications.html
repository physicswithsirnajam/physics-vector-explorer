Why do the particles separate?

INERTIAL FRAME

Inward acceleration

Sample particles moving with the rotor require a net inward force for circular motion. The acceleration at radius r is a = ω²r.

ROTATING FRAME

Relative drift

Differences in density, buoyancy-like effects, and drag lead to different radial motion relative to the rotating fluid: denser constituents move toward larger radii, lighter constituents toward smaller radii.

REAL APPLICATIONS

Cream and samples

Cream separators and laboratory centrifuges use rotation to separate mixtures; the tube, sample, and actual flow pattern determine the detailed process.
