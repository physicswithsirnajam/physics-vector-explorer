Faraday Cage Demonstration

A conducting enclosure redistributes free charge in response to an external electrostatic field. The test-charge probe is treated as too small to disturb the field.

In electrostatic equilibrium, a closed ideal conductor shields its cavity from an external static electric field (when no charge is inside the cavity). This is a conceptual, not engineering-grade shielding, model.
