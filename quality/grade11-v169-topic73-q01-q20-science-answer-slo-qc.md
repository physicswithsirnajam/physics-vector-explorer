### Source-literacy correction: the textbook's own figure

NBF p.122 **Figure 7.14** shows **weight horizontally and extension vertically**. The actual work integral is `W=∫F dx`, i.e. the area under the curve of **force vertically versus extension horizontally**. The original textbook's triangular area has the same numerical expression only for the **straight-line relation through zero**, but that graphical orientation is unsafe to generalize to nonlinear loading.

The source Word's own teacher-accuracy notes already flag this error, and our **V163 public unscored learning lesson** uses the correct orientation. The review therefore protects the author from a false “answer wrong” finding while still requiring an unambiguous scored presentation.
