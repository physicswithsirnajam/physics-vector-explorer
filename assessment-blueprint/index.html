02 · Board constraints

What makes it FBISE-aligned?

  • 1
    Summative theory SLOs only
    Exclude formative and PBA-only SLOs from the final theory paper.
  • 2
    Content-domain representation
    Include the relevant Grade XI domains, not just Unit 1. The model ToS is an example, not a fixed unit-wise allocation policy.
  • 3
    Knowledge / Understanding / Application
    Overall target 30% / 50% / 20%, with ±5 percentage points permitted. Apply to the complete paper; do not force these percentages on every unit.
  • 4
    Proper internal OR alternatives
    Both options should be comparable in difficulty and mapped to different SLOs.
  • 5
    Reviewed answers and verified sources
    Printed textbook-page references, accurate solutions, distractor checks, stimulus integrity and teacher QC.

Source: user-provided FBISE Grade XI framework/model paper, PDF pp. 28 and 51; source SLO classification table on PDF p. 5.
