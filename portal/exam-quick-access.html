A practical daily revision routine

  1. Choose one unit and review a short concept or worked example.
  2. Practise five to ten MCQs, then read the explanations for missed questions.
  3. Return to the relevant short/long answers or numerical method and try again independently.
  4. Sign in to save only eligible Unit 1 or Unit 2 practice results. Other multi-unit scores remain local/printable, not automatically saved.
