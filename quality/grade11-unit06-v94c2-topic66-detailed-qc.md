## Part B — all 56 source questions / physics findings

1. **Atomizer Q1–8:** A bulb/air-jet atomizer works by fast air near a liquid-tube mouth lowering local static pressure and allowing higher reservoir pressure to push liquid upward; **droplet breakup is distinct from suction**. Source Q6 supplementary `ΔP≈ρ_l gH` estimates static lift head only. Avoid claiming every modern mechanical *pump* perfume bottle or paint sprayer operates by this same air-jet suction mechanism. The A4 sheets demo Q8 is **supporting formative physics**, not a standalone summative B39 paper item.
2. **Filter pump Q9–16:** Water jet entrains vessel air, creating partial rather than perfect vacuum. Ideal pressure-speed association explains the *trend*; real jet mixing, vapor pressure, leaks and losses matter. Do not invent an exact filter-pump evacuation-rate formula from the 1D Bernoulli relation.
3. **Torricelli Q17–24:** `v=√(2gh)` follows only after setting surface/outlet pressures equal to atmosphere, using a *large open tank* with negligible surface speed and ignoring losses. The vertical head is free surface minus opening height, not slant distance. Real jets do not automatically attain the ideal discharge.
4. **Venturi Q25–32:** Combine steady incompressible continuity `A1 v1=A2 v2` with same-elevation Bernoulli `P1−P2=½ρ(v2²−v1²)`; piezometer relation `ΔP=ρgH` requires **same flowing liquid in both open measuring tubes at common atmospheric reference**. Do not apply that relation unchanged to a two-fluid manometer. A diameter ratio must be **squared** to obtain an area ratio; an area ratio is then squared again in the pressure-drop bracket.
5. **Aerofoil Q33–40:** Airflow/pressure distribution, momentum transfer and circulation all contribute to lift. The source correctly rejects the equal-transit-time myth and labels `F≈½ρ A(v_upper²−v_lower²)` a **simplified uniform ΔP classroom estimate**, not engineering wing certification. Crucially the **printed-source Assignment6.4 airspeed values 450 m/s and 410 m/s (Q55)** are **too high for the constant-density/incompressible-air approximation to represent physical real-wing lift** (both speeds are around/surpass typical sound speed). Its **998,460 N arithmetic is correct within the toy formula** but must be visibly called a formal exercise, not a credible real-flight performance prediction. This is an independent physics/model-validity finding; it does not change the original NBF-reported key.
6. **Magnus Q41–48:** Spin-induced asymmetric flow can produce transverse aerodynamic force on spinning balls, with direction depending on spin and travel. No universal Magnus force law is derived in the original-prepared chapter material; do not fabricate one or claim spin alone uniquely determines its direction regardless of flow geometry.
7. **Numerics Q49–56:** Independent computations reproduce **9.90 m/s**, **1.25 m**, **2.0 m/s and 1.50 kPa**, **16.0 kPa**, **0.723 / 2.89 m/s**, **80.0 kPa**, **998,460 N (formal model)** and **1500 N**. The teacher source explicitly notes that original NBF printed wording/answer keys must be verified for exactly-labelled Q54–55 before advertising an “original textbook” reproduction.

## Part C — derivations, worked numericals, formulas and limitations

- **§§1–3:** Bernoulli relations and signed vertical-height/pressure rearrangements are algebraically coherent. Define `ΔP` context each time (inlet-minus-throat vs below-minus-above); never blindly use a single sign convention across different devices.
- **§4 Torricelli:** The derivation cancels equal atmospheric pressure and neglects reservoir surface velocity under `A_tank≫A_orifice`, yielding `v=√(2gH)`. Supplementary `Q=Av` is a **model volumetric flow**, not guaranteed real flow coefficient.
- **§5 Venturi:** The sequential chain `ΔP=½ρ(v2²−v1²)`, `v2=(A1/A2)v1`, `ΔP=ρgH` under same-liquid open piezometers gives `v1=√[2gH/((A1/A2)²−1)]`. If areas are equal the ratio-form denominator is zero, so the expression cannot infer `v1` from no measurable Venturi pressure drop.
- **§6 atomizer/filter pump:** Correctly labels atomizer static head estimate **explanatory extension**, filter-pump real entrainment not captured by exact one-dimensional Bernoulli. Reservoir, tubing, surface effects may matter.
- **§7 wing model / §8 Magnus:** A simplified uniform-pressure difference is not a derivation of complete lift; air above and below is not required to reunite simultaneously. **Supplementary physical warning about high/compressible aircraft speeds** is compulsory for presenting NBF Assignment6.4 as a real-world prediction. No unwarranted “Magnus law” is claimed.
- **§9, fifteen numerical applications 9.1–9.15:** Independent arithmetic agrees with prepared results (see test). **9.11** original-labelled water-pipe Example6.4 gives `P2=80.0 kPa`; **9.12** original-labelled Assignment6.4 gives formal `ΔP=22,188 Pa`, `F=998,460 N`. **9.13–9.15** are safer explicitly **supplementary** idealized lift/atomizer values rather than NBF-exact source exercises.
- **§§10–13:** Units `Pa=N/m²`, head `m`, velocity `m/s`, `cm²→m²`, `L→m³`, consistent pressure reference are essential. Ten self-checks use simplified models; **B39 Formative** should remain distinct from summative B40/B44 assessments.
