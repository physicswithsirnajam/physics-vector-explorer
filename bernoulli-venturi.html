Magnus effect: a spinning ball

A moving, spinning ball may experience a force transverse to its motion because of the flow it creates around itself.

A deliberately simplified direction diagram for a ball moving right. Real Magnus force direction and magnitude depend on spin orientation, airflow and boundary-layer behaviour.

Venturi meter and filter pump

The throat of a Venturi meter is narrower. A pressure difference between the wide and narrow sections helps determine flow rate. A filter pump can use a fast water jet and its associated local pressure drop to entrain air.

P₁ − P₂ = ½ρ(v₂² − v₁²)   (horizontal ideal-flow model)

Aerofoil

Airflow around a wing produces a pressure distribution and a net aerodynamic force. The textbook uses the simplified higher-speed / lower-pressure illustration. Actual aerodynamic lift also involves changes in airflow momentum, circulation and angle of attack.
