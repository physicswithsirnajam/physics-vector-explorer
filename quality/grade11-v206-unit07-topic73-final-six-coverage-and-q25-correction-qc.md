### Graph and elastic-range qualification

The original NBF Fig7.14 (printed p122) places **weight/force on the horizontal axis** and **extension on the vertical axis**; the general work integral is `W=∫F dx`, which is the area under a *force-vertical versus extension-horizontal* graph. The textbook's `½Fx` **linear origin-start triangle** is correctly numerical, but the reversed-axis area is not universal work for nonlinear loading. Also, past yield, recoverable strain energy differs from total plastic loading work. The source p123 formula `U=½kx²` rests on Hookean elastic conditions, not arbitrary permanent deformation.
