How four stages connect

1. Hot isothermal expansion

Heat enters; gas expands and does positive work; ΔU = 0.

2. Adiabatic expansion

Q = 0; gas continues expanding and cools from Tₕ to T𝚌.

3. Cold isothermal compression

Heat leaves the gas; surroundings compress it at T𝚌.

4. Adiabatic compression

Q = 0; compression raises its temperature back to Tₕ.

Common misconceptions

Never divide Celsius temperatures.

Convert to kelvin before calculating any Carnot efficiency.

High efficiency ≠ high output by itself.

Work also depends on the heat input: W = ηQₕ.

Reversibility is an ideal limit.

Real processes involve finite-rate heat transfer, friction and other irreversibilities.
