FRAMEWORK CLASSIFICATION MATTERS

2. SLOs we must not incorrectly insert into Unit 4

  • P-11-B-11 — Formative: conservation of linear momentum and simple collisions. The supplied framework explicitly marks this as Formative, not summative written-theory examination content.
  • P-11-B-12 — Summative, but different topic: elastic collisions, momentum and kinetic energy. It is a linear-momentum/collision SLO, not part of the Unit 4 rotational-motion chapter.
  • P-11-B-13 — Summative, but different topic: momentum in a closed system and possible energy loss. It likewise belongs to the earlier collision/momentum learning, not Unit 4.

Therefore the Unit 4 summative coverage set is B-07 through B-10, then B-14 through B-21. Numerically adjacent framework codes are not automatically Unit 4 content. The mapping follows both the book's Unit 4 opening SLOs and the framework classifications.

3. Proposed FBISE-style Unit 4 classroom practice pack

To continue our consistent assessment workflow, the following can be used as a unit-only practice target, with the final item choices and distribution checked against the book and framework before publication.

80 × 0.540 marks: 56 independent plus 24 self-contained stimulus-based MCQs
7 × 428 marks: descriptive SRQs with balanced internal OR alternatives
2 × 612 marks: descriptive ERQs with appropriate internal OR alternatives

80 marks total. These are proposed classroom practice proportions inspired by the current FBISE 50%-MCQ model and our existing unit-specific practice workflow. They are not an official allocation of 80 questions to Unit 4 in the full HSSC-I examination. No completed exam paper, verified answer key, OMR, graded test or protected attempt is added in this V38 audit.

  1. Draft questions only from the mapped 12 summative theory SLOs and textbook pp.53–76. Confirm facts, formulas, numeric answers and the relationship between scenario text and its MCQs.
  2. Use the familiar boxed FBISE options and A/B/C/D bubbles when later generating print editions, with non-predictable placement of correct options.
  3. Independently review each MCQ and every internal OR answer, explanation, mark split, SLO tag and cognitive level before it becomes student-facing.
  4. Keep Unit 1–3 assessments, Unit 4 learning resources, existing login and saved student progress unchanged while new Unit 4 practice files are developed.

4. Final audit statement

Teaching-source mapping: Complete for 12 of 12 summative Unit 4 SLOs once Part 5 is released.

Published assessment-item mapping: Still zero. A full question bank and descriptive marking scheme require a separate authoring and teacher-QC stage.

Lab coverage: Radians and rotating wheel; centripetal force and banking; satellite weightlessness and artificial gravity; inertia/torque/angular momentum; conceptual centrifugation. These are teaching models with stated assumptions, not certified exam scoring systems.
