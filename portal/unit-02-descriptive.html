Part A — Short responses (9 textbook-labelled)

TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 1 · P-11-B-02

If the cross product of two vectors vanishes, what can be concluded about their orientation?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Use the definition
    |A⃗ × B⃗| = AB sin θ
  2. For two non-zero vectors
    If AB ≠ 0 and the cross product vanishes, sin θ = 0.
  3. State both possible directions
    θ = 0° or 180°, so the vectors are parallel or antiparallel.

Presentation guidance: If either vector is the zero vector, its direction cannot be inferred from the vanishing cross product.

TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 2 · P-11-B-02

Find the dot products of Cartesian unit vectors with each other at 0° and 90°.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Scalar product of unit vectors
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB cos θ, with A = B = 1.
  2. Parallel unit vectors
    î · î = ĵ · ĵ = k̂ · k̂ = cos 0° = 1.
  3. Perpendicular unit vectors
    î · ĵ = ĵ · k̂ = k̂ · î = cos 90° = 0.
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 3 · P-11-B-02

Show that the scalar product obeys the commutative property.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Write the definition in the first order
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB cos θ
  2. Reverse the vectors
    B⃗ · A⃗ = BA cos θ
  3. Use AB = BA
    A⃗ · B⃗ = B⃗ · A⃗, hence the dot product is commutative.
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 4 · P-11-B-02

Evaluate (a) î · (ĵ × k̂) and (b) ĵ × (ĵ × k̂).

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. First simplify the inner product
    ĵ × k̂ = î
  2. Part (a)
    î · (ĵ × k̂) = î · î = 1
  3. Part (b)
    ĵ × (ĵ × k̂) = ĵ × î = −k̂
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 5 · P-11-B-02

If both the dot and the cross products of two vectors are zero, what can be concluded?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Dot product for non-zero vectors
    A⃗ · B⃗ = 0 implies perpendicular orientation.
  2. Cross product for non-zero vectors
    A⃗ × B⃗ = 0 implies parallel or antiparallel orientation.
  3. Combine the conditions
    Both cannot hold for the same two non-zero vectors. Therefore at least one vector must be the null vector.
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 6 · P-11-B-01

What are rectangular components of a vector, and how are they found?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Definition
    Rectangular components lie along two mutually perpendicular axes.
  2. For a vector of magnitude A making angle θ with +x
    Aₓ = A cos θ; Aᵧ = A sin θ
  3. Write vector form
    A⃗ = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ. The signs depend on the quadrant.
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 7 · P-11-B-02

Give two examples each of scalar and vector products.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Scalar products
    Work: W = F⃗ · d⃗; Power: P = F⃗ · v⃗
  2. Vector products
    Torque: τ⃗ = r⃗ × F⃗; Angular momentum: L⃗ = r⃗ × p⃗
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 8 · P-11-B-02

Show that A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Express both vectors
    A⃗ = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ + A_zk̂; B⃗ = Bₓî + Bᵧĵ + B_zk̂
  2. Expand their dot product
    Each matching unit-vector product equals 1 and each perpendicular cross term equals 0.
  3. Collect remaining terms
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z
TEXTBOOK SHORT QUESTION 9 · P-11-B-02

What units are associated with the Cartesian unit vectors î, ĵ and k̂?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Meaning of unit vectors
    They specify directions along the coordinate axes.
  2. Magnitude
    |î| = |ĵ| = |k̂| = 1
  3. Units
    Unit vectors are dimensionless and have no physical units.

Part A2 — Additional SLO short responses (Q10–Q35)

Twenty-six additional questions from Stage 3, in original source order. Q10–Q17 address component resolution; Q18–Q35 address scalar and vector products.

ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 10 · P-11-B-01

What is meant by resolving a vector?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Define resolution
    Replace a vector by two or more component vectors whose combined effect equals the original vector.
  2. For rectangular components in two dimensions
    A⃗ = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ; the component directions are mutually perpendicular.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 11 · P-11-B-01

Derive expressions for the rectangular components of a vector.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Consider vector A making angle θ with the positive x-axis
    The vector and its rectangular components form a right-angled triangle.
  2. Horizontal component
    cos θ = Aₓ/A ⇒ Aₓ = A cos θ.
  3. Vertical component
    sin θ = Aᵧ/A ⇒ Aᵧ = A sin θ.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 12 · P-11-B-01

How can the magnitude and direction of a vector be found from its rectangular components?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Apply Pythagoras
    A² = Aₓ² + Aᵧ² ⇒ A = √(Aₓ² + Aᵧ²).
  2. Calculate the direction
    tan θ = Aᵧ/Aₓ; θ = tan⁻¹(Aᵧ/Aₓ).
  3. Check signs
    Select the physical quadrant from the signs of Aₓ and Aᵧ rather than using the inverse tangent uncritically.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 13 · P-11-B-01

A vector is A⃗ = 3î + 4ĵ. Find its magnitude and direction relative to the x-axis.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Magnitude
    A = √(3² + 4²) = 5.
  2. Direction
    tan θ = 4/3 ⇒ θ = tan⁻¹(4/3) ≈ 53°.
  3. Final statement
    A = 5 and θ ≈ 53° measured anticlockwise from +x.

Source note: The Stage 3 source connects this application with FBISE model-paper SLO P-11-B-01.

ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 14 · P-11-B-01

A force of 10 N makes an angle of 60° with the positive x-axis. Find its rectangular components.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Horizontal component
    Fₓ = F cos 60° = 10(0.5) = 5 N.
  2. Vertical component
    Fᵧ = F sin 60° = 10(0.866) ≈ 8.66 N.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 15 · P-11-B-01

The rectangular components of a vector are 8 units and 6 units. Find its magnitude and direction.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Magnitude
    A = √(8² + 6²) = √100 = 10 units.
  2. Direction
    tan θ = 6/8 = 0.75 ⇒ θ = tan⁻¹(0.75) ≈ 36.9°.
  3. Final answer
    A = 10 units; θ ≈ 36.9°.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 16 · P-11-B-01

Write the position vector of point P(6,8) and find its magnitude.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Position vector
    r⃗ = 6î + 8ĵ.
  2. Magnitude
    |r⃗| = √(6² + 8²) = √100 = 10.
  3. Final statement
    r⃗ = 6î + 8ĵ; |r⃗| = 10.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 17 · P-11-B-01

Why are rectangular components useful in vector calculations?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Component form
    A⃗ = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ describes the same vector along mutually perpendicular axes.
  2. Main advantages
    They allow vectors to be compared along x/y axes, simplify vector operations, and allow magnitude and direction to be found.
  3. Application
    They make two-dimensional physics problems easier to analyse.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 18 · P-11-B-02

Define scalar product of two vectors.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Definition
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB cos θ, where θ is the angle between the vectors.
  2. Nature of result
    The dot product is a scalar.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 19 · P-11-B-02

Explain the geometrical meaning of the dot product.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Resolve a projection
    A cos θ is the scalar component of A⃗ along B⃗.
  2. Multiply by the second magnitude
    A⃗ · B⃗ = B(A cos θ).
  3. Interpretation
    The product equals the magnitude of one vector multiplied by the scalar component of the other along it.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 20 · P-11-B-02

State the important special cases of the scalar product.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Parallel vectors, θ = 0°
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB.
  2. Perpendicular vectors, θ = 90°
    A⃗ · B⃗ = 0.
  3. Antiparallel vectors, θ = 180°
    A⃗ · B⃗ = −AB.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 21 · P-11-B-02

Define vector product of two vectors.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Definition
    A⃗ × B⃗ = AB sin θ n̂.
  2. Direction
    n̂ is a unit vector perpendicular to the plane containing A⃗ and B⃗.
  3. Nature of result
    A vector product yields a vector.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 22 · P-11-B-02

How is the direction of a cross product determined?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Apply the right-hand rule
    Curl the fingers of the right hand from the first vector A⃗ toward the second vector B⃗ through the smaller angle.
  2. Read the direction
    The extended thumb indicates the direction of A⃗ × B⃗, perpendicular to their plane.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 23 · P-11-B-02

State the important special cases of the vector product.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Parallel or antiparallel
    For θ = 0° or 180°, A⃗ × B⃗ = 0.
  2. Perpendicular
    For θ = 90°, |A⃗ × B⃗| = AB, the maximum cross-product magnitude.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 24 · P-11-B-02

Distinguish scalar and vector products on the basis of their commutative properties.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Scalar (dot) product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = B⃗ · A⃗: commutative.
  2. Vector (cross) product
    A⃗ × B⃗ = −(B⃗ × A⃗): anti-commutative.
  3. Direction consequence
    Reversing the order reverses the vector-product direction.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 25 · P-11-B-02

Write the dot product of two vectors in rectangular-component form.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Write both vectors
    A⃗ = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ + A_zk̂; B⃗ = Bₓî + Bᵧĵ + B_zk̂.
  2. Multiply and combine matching components
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 26 · P-11-B-02

Calculate the dot product of A⃗ = 2î + 3ĵ and B⃗ = 4î − ĵ.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Use the component formula
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ.
  2. Substitute
    A⃗ · B⃗ = (2)(4) + (3)(−1) = 8 − 3 = 5.
  3. Answer
    A⃗ · B⃗ = 5.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 27 · P-11-B-02

Find the angle between A⃗ = î + ĵ and B⃗ = î.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Find the dot product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = (1)(1) + (1)(0) = 1.
  2. Find magnitudes
    |A⃗| = √2; |B⃗| = 1.
  3. Solve for the angle
    cos θ = 1/√2 ⇒ θ = 45°.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 28 · P-11-B-02

Given A⃗ = 5î + ĵ and B⃗ = 2î + 4ĵ, find the scalar projection of A on B and of B on A.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Calculate the dot product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = (5)(2) + (1)(4) = 14.
  2. Calculate magnitudes
    |A⃗| = √26; |B⃗| = √20.
  3. Scalar projection of A on B
    comp_B A = 14/√20 ≈ 3.13.
  4. Scalar projection of B on A
    comp_A B = 14/√26 ≈ 2.75.

Source note: The source connects this problem with textbook Assignment 2.2. Scalar projection differs from the vector projection.

ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 29 · P-11-B-02

Calculate A⃗ × B⃗ for A⃗ = 2î + 3ĵ and B⃗ = 4î − ĵ.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Use the two-dimensional cross-product formula
    A⃗ × B⃗ = (AₓBᵧ − AᵧBₓ)k̂.
  2. Substitute
    A⃗ × B⃗ = [2(−1) − 3(4)]k̂ = (−2 − 12)k̂.
  3. Answer
    A⃗ × B⃗ = −14k̂.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 30 · P-11-B-02

Write the cross-product relations among the Cartesian unit vectors.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Cyclic products
    î × ĵ = k̂; ĵ × k̂ = î; k̂ × î = ĵ.
  2. Reverse order reverses sign
    ĵ × î = −k̂; k̂ × ĵ = −î; î × k̂ = −ĵ.
  3. Self-products
    î × î = ĵ × ĵ = k̂ × k̂ = 0.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 31 · P-11-B-02

Why does reversing the order of vectors in a cross product change its sign?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Magnitudes are unchanged
    Both ordered cross products have magnitude AB sin θ.
  2. Directions reverse
    The right-hand rule points in the opposite perpendicular direction if the order is reversed.
  3. Conclude
    B⃗ × A⃗ = −(A⃗ × B⃗).
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 32 · P-11-B-02

How does the cross product give the area of a parallelogram?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Cross-product magnitude
    |A⃗ × B⃗| = AB sin θ.
  2. Geometric area
    The parallelogram has base A and perpendicular height B sin θ.
  3. Compare
    Area = A(B sin θ) = |A⃗ × B⃗|.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 33 · P-11-B-02

What conclusion can be drawn if both A⃗ · B⃗ = 0 and A⃗ × B⃗ = 0?

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. If both vectors were non-zero
    A zero dot product requires perpendicular vectors; a zero cross product requires parallel or antiparallel vectors.
  2. Both cannot hold simultaneously
    The same two non-zero vectors cannot be both perpendicular and parallel/antiparallel.
  3. Conclusion
    At least one vector is the null vector.
ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 34 · P-11-B-02

If |A⃗ · B⃗| = |A⃗ × B⃗|, find the angle between the vectors.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Write the magnitude condition for non-zero vectors
    AB|cos θ| = AB|sin θ| ⇒ |cos θ| = |sin θ|.
  2. Textbook/FBISE-style intended answer
    Under the acute-angle convention, cos θ = sin θ ⇒ tan θ = 1 ⇒ θ = 45°.
  3. Important mathematical clarification
    For a general angle 0° ≤ θ ≤ 180° with both absolute-value signs, θ = 45° or 135° both satisfy the equation.

Source note: Preserve the source’s QC-05 clarification: the model-paper option intends 45°, but the full mathematical interpretation also admits 135°. Do not suppress either statement.

ADDITIONAL SLO SHORT QUESTION 35 · P-11-B-02

Using the commutative property, distinguish between scalar product and vector product of two vectors. Give mathematical justification.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Scalar (dot) product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB cos θ = BA cos θ = B⃗ · A⃗. Its result is scalar and the order does not matter.
  2. Vector (cross) product
    A⃗ × B⃗ = AB sin θ n̂; reversing vector order reverses n̂.
  3. Anti-commutative result
    A⃗ × B⃗ = −(B⃗ × A⃗). Its result is a vector.

Source note: The source identifies this as matching the FBISE descriptive-question format for comparing both products.

Part B — Comprehensive responses (4 textbook-labelled)

TEXTBOOK COMPREHENSIVE QUESTION 1 · P-11-B-01

Explain the resolution of a vector in two dimensions into rectangular components.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Define resolution
    Replace one vector by components along two perpendicular directions, x and y.
  2. Represent the vector
    A⃗ = Aₓî + Aᵧĵ
  3. Resolve horizontally
    cos θ = Aₓ/A ⇒ Aₓ = A cos θ
  4. Resolve vertically
    sin θ = Aᵧ/A ⇒ Aᵧ = A sin θ
  5. Reconstruct the vector
    A = √(Aₓ² + Aᵧ²) and tan θ = Aᵧ/Aₓ. Choose the correct quadrant from component signs.

Presentation guidance: In a written response, draw labelled +x and +y axes, the vector A⃗ at angle θ, and perpendicular projections Aₓ and Aᵧ.

TEXTBOOK COMPREHENSIVE QUESTION 2 · P-11-B-02

Describe the scalar (dot) product of two vectors and explain its properties.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Definition and result
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB cos θ. The result is a scalar.
  2. Geometric interpretation
    A⃗ · B⃗ = B(A cos θ): one magnitude times the other vector’s scalar projection.
  3. Commutative law
    A⃗ · B⃗ = B⃗ · A⃗
  4. Special orientations
    Parallel: AB; perpendicular: 0; antiparallel: −AB; self-product: A².
  5. Component rule
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z
  6. Applications
    Work W = F⃗ · d⃗; Power P = F⃗ · v⃗; electric flux uses a dot product of field and area vector.
TEXTBOOK COMPREHENSIVE QUESTION 3 · P-11-B-02

Describe the vector (cross) product of two vectors and explain its properties.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Definition and result
    A⃗ × B⃗ = AB sin θ n̂, where n̂ is perpendicular to the plane of A⃗ and B⃗.
  2. Direction
    Use the right-hand rule to determine the direction of n̂.
  3. Anti-commutative law
    A⃗ × B⃗ = −(B⃗ × A⃗)
  4. Special orientations
    Parallel or antiparallel: zero; perpendicular: maximum magnitude AB; A⃗ × A⃗ = 0.
  5. Unit-vector identities
    î × ĵ = k̂; ĵ × k̂ = î; k̂ × î = ĵ; reversing the order reverses the sign.
  6. Component form
    A⃗ × B⃗ = (AᵧB_z−A_zBᵧ)î + (A_zBₓ−AₓB_z)ĵ + (AₓBᵧ−AᵧBₓ)k̂
  7. Applications
    Torque τ⃗ = r⃗ × F⃗; angular momentum L⃗ = r⃗ × p⃗.
TEXTBOOK COMPREHENSIVE QUESTION 4 · P-11-B-02

What is the geometric interpretation of the cross product? Explain with a diagram.

Reveal model answer and reasoning
  1. Start with two vectors
    Adjacent sides of a parallelogram have magnitudes A and B and included angle θ.
  2. Identify base and perpendicular height
    Base = A; height = B sin θ.
  3. Calculate its area
    Area = A(B sin θ) = AB sin θ.
  4. Connect the result to vector product magnitude
    |A⃗ × B⃗| = AB sin θ, therefore the parallelogram area is |A⃗ × B⃗|.
  5. State direction separately
    The cross-product vector points perpendicular to the parallelogram plane according to the right-hand rule.
