    3. Strategy for exam-quality answers

    • State the relationship: components use cosine and sine, scalar products use cosine, and cross-product magnitudes use sine.
    • Use signs: a vector in Quadrant II, III or IV may have negative components; the signed k̂ cross-product coefficient also carries direction information.
    • Check angles: 135° is obtuse. A dot product can be negative even though cross-product magnitude is positive.
    • Show full working: compare with the Unit 2 worked numericals and descriptive model answers before finalizing a written response.
