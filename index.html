For Sir Najam's classroom and student website

Teacher projection routine (8–12 minutes)

  • Ask students for a prediction before changing the angle.
  • Move the glowing arrow tip, pause at 90°, 180° and 270°, and discuss signs.
  • Use the dot/cross tabs to contrast products at 0°, 90° and 180°.
  • Let students solve one numerical independently before revealing the live result.

Student self-study routine

  • Experiment for two minutes and write down observations.
  • Complete the three prediction challenges in each tab.
  • Answer the concept-check question and explain the result.
  • Use the printable investigation sheet for written work.

Instructions: Use the interactive Vector Explorer and show calculations with correct mathematical signs. Write your own explanation for each observation.

  1. Set A = 10 N and θ = 30°. Calculate Aₓ and Aᵧ using trigonometric functions. Then verify them on the screen.
  2. At which two angles from 0° to 360° is Aₓ = 0? Explain your reasoning.
  3. Set A = 10 units and B = 8 units. Find A·B at θ = 0°, 90° and 180°.
  4. With the same magnitudes, find A × B (z-component) at θ = 90° and 270°. State the direction in each case.
  5. Explain why A·B is a scalar but A × B is a vector, and give one property of each.
