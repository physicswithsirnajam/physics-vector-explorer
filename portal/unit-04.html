FBISE source and assessment boundaries

Primary source: NBF Grade XI Physics, Unit 4 — Rotational and Circular Motion, printed p.53 chapter opening and pp.54–59 rotational kinematics. Official assessment classifications follow the supplied FBISE HSSC-I Physics Assessment Framework.

  • P-11-B-07 — Summative (covered): Express angles in radians.
  • P-11-B-08 — Summative (covered): Calculate angular displacement, angular velocity, angular acceleration and related linear/angular quantities.
  • P-11-B-09 — Summative (covered): Apply equations of rotational motion to problems.
  • P-11-B-10 — Summative (next stage): Qualitative analysis of motion in a curved path due to a perpendicular force.
  • P-11-B-14 onward — Summative (later stages): Centripetal force and applications, artificial gravity, torque, moment of inertia, angular momentum and related topics.

This release intentionally does not claim complete chapter coverage, a summative question bank or verified full-syllabus exam readiness. The simulations run locally without saving student progress or accessing enrolled accounts.
