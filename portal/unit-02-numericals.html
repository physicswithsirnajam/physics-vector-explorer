Part A — Five textbook-labelled numericals

Textbook numerical 2.1 · P-11-B-02

Angle from the cross-to-dot ratio

Question: The magnitude of the cross product is √3 times the dot product of two vectors. Find the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Start with the relationship
    |A⃗ × B⃗| = √3(A⃗ · B⃗)
  2. Substitute the definitions
    AB sin θ = √3 AB cos θ
  3. Cancel AB for non-zero vectors and rearrange
    tan θ = √3
  4. Find the required angle
    θ = tan⁻¹(√3) = 60°

Final answer: θ = 60°

Examiner’s tip: Because the dot product here is positive, the acute angle is consistent with the given relation.

Textbook numerical 2.2 · P-11-B-01

Force magnitude from its horizontal component

Question: A force acts 30° above the horizontal. Its horizontal component is 20 N. Determine the force magnitude.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Choose the component formula
    Fₓ = F cos θ
  2. Substitute the given values
    20 = F cos 30°
  3. Rearrange and calculate
    F = 20 / 0.866 ≈ 23.1 N

Final answer: F ≈ 23.1 N

Examiner’s tip: Do not confuse the 20 N component with the full force.

Textbook numerical 2.3 · P-11-B-02

Dot and cross products of two inclined vectors

Question: A force vector of 5.5 N makes 10° with the x-axis, and a position vector of 4.3 m makes 80° with the x-axis. Determine the dot product and magnitude of the cross product.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. First determine the angle between the vectors
    θ = 80° − 10° = 70°
  2. Multiply the magnitudes
    Fr = (5.5)(4.3) = 23.65 N·m
  3. Calculate the scalar product
    F⃗ · r⃗ = 23.65 cos 70° ≈ 8.1 N·m
  4. Calculate the vector-product magnitude
    |F⃗ × r⃗| = 23.65 sin 70° ≈ 22.2 N·m

Final answer: F⃗ · r⃗ ≈ 8.1 N·m; |F⃗ × r⃗| ≈ 22.2 N·m

Examiner’s tip: Source-QC note: the position-vector magnitude is 4.3 m, not 3 m. Use the angle between the two vectors, not either angle measured from x alone.

Textbook numerical 2.4 · P-11-B-02

Angle from known scalar and vector products

Question: Two vectors have dot product 6√3 and cross-product magnitude 6. Determine the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Write the two given relationships
    AB cos θ = 6√3; AB sin θ = 6
  2. Divide sine relationship by cosine relationship
    tan θ = 6/(6√3) = 1/√3
  3. Evaluate the angle
    θ = tan⁻¹(1/√3) = 30°

Final answer: θ = 30°

Examiner’s tip: The positive dot product fixes the angle on the acute side.

Textbook numerical 2.5 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a 200 N force into x and y components

Question: A force of 200 N makes 30° with the positive x-axis. Find its horizontal and vertical components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Horizontal component
    Fₓ = F cos θ = 200 cos 30° = 173.2 N
  2. Vertical component
    Fᵧ = F sin θ = 200 sin 30° = 100 N
  3. Check the signs
    Both components are positive for a vector 30° above +x.

Final answer: Fₓ = 173.2 N; Fᵧ = 100 N

Examiner’s tip: Use cosine for the adjacent/horizontal component and sine for the opposite/vertical component when θ is measured from +x.

Part B — First eight additional SLO applications

Additional SLO practice 6 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a 40 N force at 25°

Question: A force of 40 N acts at 25° above the positive x-axis. Find its rectangular components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Find x-component
    Fₓ = 40 cos 25° ≈ 36.3 N
  2. Find y-component
    Fᵧ = 40 sin 25° ≈ 16.9 N
  3. Check sign convention
    Both components are positive in the first quadrant.

Final answer: Fₓ ≈ 36.3 N; Fᵧ ≈ 16.9 N

Examiner’s tip: State the unit N for both components.

Additional SLO practice 8 · P-11-B-01

Recover a Quadrant-II vector’s magnitude and direction

Question: Given A⃗ = −6î + 8ĵ, find its magnitude and direction measured anticlockwise from +x.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use Pythagoras for the magnitude
    A = √[(-6)² + 8²] = 10
  2. Find the reference angle
    α = tan⁻¹(8/6) ≈ 53.1°
  3. Identify Quadrant II (negative x, positive y)
    θ = 180° − 53.1° = 126.9°

Final answer: |A⃗| = 10; θ ≈ 126.9°

Examiner’s tip: Do not give 53.1° as the final direction: quadrant signs matter.

Additional SLO practice 10 · P-11-B-01

Find force magnitude and its vertical component

Question: A force makes 40° with the horizontal and has a horizontal component of 30 N. Find its magnitude and vertical component.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Resolve horizontally
    30 = F cos 40°
  2. Calculate magnitude
    F = 30/cos 40° ≈ 39.2 N
  3. Resolve vertically
    Fᵧ = F sin 40° ≈ 25.2 N

Final answer: F ≈ 39.2 N; Fᵧ ≈ 25.2 N

Examiner’s tip: Find F first, or use Fᵧ = 30 tan 40° as a check.

Additional SLO practice 16 · P-11-B-02

Dot product from three-dimensional components

Question: A⃗ = 3î − 2ĵ + 4k̂ and B⃗ = î + 5ĵ − 2k̂. Find A⃗ · B⃗.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Multiply matching components and add
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AₓBₓ + AᵧBᵧ + A_zB_z
  2. Substitute the components
    = 3(1) + (−2)(5) + 4(−2)
  3. Combine terms
    = 3 − 10 − 8 = −15

Final answer: A⃗ · B⃗ = −15

Examiner’s tip: A negative dot product means the angle between these non-zero vectors is obtuse.

Additional SLO practice 18 · P-11-B-02

Instantaneous power from force and velocity vectors

Question: F⃗ = 6î + 8ĵ N and v⃗ = 3î + 4ĵ m·s⁻¹. Find the power delivered.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Recognize the physical scalar product
    P = F⃗ · v⃗
  2. Multiply corresponding components
    P = 6(3) + 8(4)
  3. Add and write proper units
    P = 18 + 32 = 50 W

Final answer: P = 50 W

Examiner’s tip: Power is scalar; the dot product gives watts.

Additional SLO practice 22 · P-11-B-02

Cross product in three dimensions

Question: A⃗ = 2î − ĵ + 3k̂ and B⃗ = î + 4ĵ − 2k̂. Find A⃗ × B⃗ and its magnitude.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Write the Cartesian cross-product expressions
    A⃗ × B⃗ = (AᵧB_z−A_zBᵧ)î + (A_zBₓ−AₓB_z)ĵ + (AₓBᵧ−AᵧBₓ)k̂
  2. Evaluate the î-component
    (−1)(−2) − (3)(4) = −10
  3. Evaluate the ĵ-component
    (3)(1) − (2)(−2) = 7
  4. Evaluate the k̂-component
    (2)(4) − (−1)(1) = 9
  5. Find the magnitude
    √[(-10)² + 7² + 9²] = √230 ≈ 15.2

Final answer: A⃗ × B⃗ = −10î + 7ĵ + 9k̂; magnitude ≈ 15.2

Examiner’s tip: When expanding determinants, remember the sign on the ĵ term.

Additional SLO practice 25 · P-11-B-02

Obtuse angle from negative dot product

Question: Two non-zero vectors have A⃗ · B⃗ = −8 and |A⃗ × B⃗| = 8√3. Find the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the magnitudes of the products to calculate a reference angle
    tan α = |A⃗ × B⃗| / |A⃗ · B⃗| = √3
  2. Evaluate the reference angle
    α = 60°
  3. Account for the negative dot product
    cos θ < 0, so θ is obtuse: θ = 180° − 60°

Final answer: θ = 120°

Examiner’s tip: A frequent error is 60°. The negative dot product makes the angle obtuse.

Additional SLO practice 30 · P-11-B-02

Integrated angle and product-of-magnitudes challenge

Question: For two non-zero vectors, A⃗ · B⃗ = 18 and |A⃗ × B⃗| = 24. Find their angle and AB.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Calculate the angle
    tan θ = 24/18 = 4/3; θ ≈ 53.1°
  2. Use the dot/cross identity
    (AB)² = (A⃗ · B⃗)² + |A⃗ × B⃗|²
  3. Substitute the values
    (AB)² = 18² + 24² = 900
  4. Take the positive square root
    AB = 30

Final answer: θ ≈ 53.1°; AB = 30

Examiner’s tip: AB is the product of magnitudes, not the dot product.

Part C — Remaining 17 additional SLO applications

This section completes the Stage 5 additional numerical sequence. The source question numbers are preserved, and the earlier thirteen published problem links remain unchanged.

Additional SLO practice 7 · P-11-B-01

Reconstruct a vector from its components

Question: The rectangular components of a vector are Aₓ = 9 and Aᵧ = 12. Find its magnitude and direction.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Apply Pythagoras
    A = √(9² + 12²) = √225 = 15
  2. Find the direction
    tan θ = Aᵧ/Aₓ = 12/9
  3. Evaluate
    θ = tan⁻¹(12/9) ≈ 53.1°

Final answer: A = 15; θ ≈ 53.1°

Examiner’s tip: Both components are positive, so the direction lies in Quadrant I.

Additional SLO practice 9 · P-11-B-01

Position vector from point coordinates

Question: A point has coordinates P(−3, 4). Find its position vector, magnitude and direction.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Write the position vector
    r⃗ = −3î + 4ĵ
  2. Find its magnitude
    |r⃗| = √[(-3)² + 4²] = 5
  3. Correct the reference angle for Quadrant II
    θ = 180° − tan⁻¹(4/3) ≈ 126.9°

Final answer: r⃗ = −3î + 4ĵ; |r⃗| = 5; θ ≈ 126.9°

Examiner’s tip: A negative x component and positive y component indicate Quadrant II.

Additional SLO practice 11 · P-11-B-01

Find a force from its vertical component

Question: A force makes an angle of 35° with the horizontal. Its vertical component is 18 N. Find the magnitude of the force and its horizontal component.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the vertical component
    Fᵧ = F sin 35° ⇒ 18 = F sin 35°
  2. Find the full force
    F = 18 / sin 35° ≈ 31.4 N
  3. Find the horizontal component
    Fₓ = F cos 35° ≈ 25.7 N

Final answer: F ≈ 31.4 N; Fₓ ≈ 25.7 N

Examiner’s tip: Use sine when the given component is opposite the angle measured from the horizontal.

Additional SLO practice 12 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a Quadrant-II vector at 120°

Question: A 15-unit vector makes an angle of 120° measured anticlockwise from the positive x-axis. Find its rectangular components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Resolve along the x-axis
    Aₓ = 15 cos 120° = −7.5
  2. Resolve along the y-axis
    Aᵧ = 15 sin 120° ≈ 13.0
  3. Verify the signs
    120° is in Quadrant II: x negative, y positive.

Final answer: Aₓ = −7.5; Aᵧ ≈ 13.0

Examiner’s tip: The vector magnitude is 15 units; components carry positive/negative signs according to direction.

Additional SLO practice 13 · P-11-B-01

Direction of a Quadrant-IV vector

Question: For A⃗ = 5î − 12ĵ, find the magnitude and direction.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Find magnitude
    |A⃗| = √[5² + (-12)²] = 13
  2. Find reference angle
    α = tan⁻¹(12/5) ≈ 67.4°
  3. Correct for Quadrant IV
    θ = −67.4° or 360° − 67.4° = 292.6°

Final answer: |A⃗| = 13; θ = −67.4° or 292.6°

Examiner’s tip: Specify whether the angle is clockwise (negative) or anticlockwise from the positive x-axis.

Additional SLO practice 14 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a vector at 150°

Question: A vector of magnitude 25 makes 150° with the positive x-axis. Find its rectangular components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Find the horizontal component
    Aₓ = 25 cos 150° ≈ −21.7
  2. Find the vertical component
    Aᵧ = 25 sin 150° = 12.5
  3. Check the quadrant
    Quadrant II has Aₓ < 0 and Aᵧ > 0.

Final answer: Aₓ ≈ −21.7; Aᵧ = 12.5

Examiner’s tip: Do not replace the 150° angle with its acute reference angle without adjusting signs.

Additional SLO practice 15 · P-11-B-01

Resolve a vector at 210°

Question: A 20-unit vector is directed at 210°. Find its x- and y-components.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Resolve horizontally
    Aₓ = 20 cos 210° ≈ −17.3
  2. Resolve vertically
    Aᵧ = 20 sin 210° = −10.0
  3. Interpret the signs
    210° lies in Quadrant III, so both components are negative.

Final answer: Aₓ ≈ −17.3; Aᵧ = −10.0

Examiner’s tip: Signs are essential; the magnitudes alone do not specify direction.

Additional SLO practice 17 · P-11-B-02

Test perpendicularity using the dot product

Question: Given A⃗ = 2î + ĵ and B⃗ = î − 2ĵ, find the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Compute the dot product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = (2)(1) + (1)(−2) = 0
  2. Apply the definition
    A⃗ · B⃗ = AB cos θ
  3. Both vectors are non-zero
    cos θ = 0 ⇒ θ = 90°

Final answer: θ = 90°; the vectors are perpendicular

Examiner’s tip: A zero dot product implies perpendicularity only when both vectors are non-zero.

Additional SLO practice 19 · P-11-B-02

Work done by an inclined force

Question: A 20 N force acts through a displacement of 5 m. The angle between the force and displacement is 60°. Find the work done.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the scalar-product definition of work
    W = Fd cos θ
  2. Substitute the values
    W = (20)(5) cos 60°
  3. Evaluate and include the unit
    W = 100(0.5) = 50 J

Final answer: W = 50 J

Examiner’s tip: Only the force component parallel to displacement does work.

Additional SLO practice 20 · P-11-B-02

Electric flux from a uniform electric field

Question: A uniform electric field E = 300 N C⁻¹ passes through an area A = 0.020 m². The angle between E⃗ and the area vector is 30°. Find the electric flux.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the scalar-product formula
    Φ = E⃗ · A⃗ = EA cos θ
  2. Substitute
    Φ = (300)(0.020) cos 30°
  3. Evaluate and state the units
    Φ ≈ 6(0.866) ≈ 5.20 N m² C⁻¹

Final answer: Φ ≈ 5.20 N m² C⁻¹

Examiner’s tip: The angle is measured with the area vector (the normal to the surface), not the surface itself.

Additional SLO practice 21 · P-11-B-02

Scalar projections of two vectors

Question: Given A⃗ = 3î + 4ĵ and B⃗ = 4î, find (1) scalar projection of A on B and (2) scalar projection of B on A.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Compute the dot product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = (3)(4) + (4)(0) = 12
  2. Find both magnitudes
    |A⃗| = √(3²+4²) = 5; |B⃗| = 4
  3. Project A on B
    comp_B A = (A⃗ · B⃗)/|B⃗| = 12/4 = 3
  4. Project B on A
    comp_A B = (A⃗ · B⃗)/|A⃗| = 12/5 = 2.4

Final answer: comp_B A = 3; comp_A B = 2.4

Examiner’s tip: The denominator is the magnitude of the vector onto which you project.

Additional SLO practice 23 · P-11-B-02

Area of the vector parallelogram

Question: Two vectors have magnitudes 8 and 5, with an angle of 30° between them. Find the area of the parallelogram they form.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use cross-product magnitude
    Area = AB sin θ
  2. Substitute values
    Area = (8)(5) sin 30°
  3. Evaluate
    Area = 40(0.5) = 20 square units

Final answer: Area = 20 square units

Examiner’s tip: Area is the magnitude of the cross product, not the dot product.

Additional SLO practice 24 · P-11-B-02

Angle from dot and cross products

Question: For two vectors, A⃗ · B⃗ = 12 and |A⃗ × B⃗| = 12√3. Find the angle between them.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Form the tangent ratio
    tan θ = |A⃗ × B⃗| / (A⃗ · B⃗)
  2. Substitute
    tan θ = 12√3 / 12 = √3
  3. Evaluate with the sign of the dot product
    θ = 60° (positive dot product, acute angle)

Final answer: θ = 60°

Examiner’s tip: Check the sign of the dot product before choosing an angle from inverse tangent.

Additional SLO practice 26 · P-11-B-02

Integrated dot, cross, angle and area

Question: Given A⃗ = î + 2ĵ and B⃗ = 3î + 4ĵ, find (1) dot product, (2) cross product, (3) angle between the vectors and (4) area of the parallelogram.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Scalar product
    A⃗ · B⃗ = (1)(3) + (2)(4) = 11
  2. Vector product
    A⃗ × B⃗ = (1×4 − 2×3)k̂ = −2k̂
  3. Magnitudes
    |A⃗| = √5; |B⃗| = 5
  4. Angle between vectors
    cos θ = 11/(5√5), hence θ ≈ 10.3°
  5. Area of parallelogram
    Area = |A⃗ × B⃗| = 2 square units

Final answer: A⃗ · B⃗ = 11; A⃗ × B⃗ = −2k̂; θ ≈ 10.3°; area = 2 square units

Examiner’s tip: The magnitude of the cross product gives area; the signed k̂ component gives orientation.

Additional SLO practice 27 · P-11-B-02

Torque due to a perpendicular force

Question: A force of 50 N acts perpendicular to a position vector of magnitude 0.40 m. Find the magnitude of the torque.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the cross-product formula
    τ = rF sin θ
  2. Perpendicular force means θ = 90°
    sin 90° = 1
  3. Substitute
    τ = (0.40)(50)(1) = 20 N m

Final answer: τ = 20 N m

Examiner’s tip: For torque magnitude the lever arm is perpendicular distance; here the force already makes a right angle.

Additional SLO practice 28 · P-11-B-02

Angular momentum from position and momentum

Question: A particle has momentum p = 3 kg m s⁻¹ at a distance r = 2 m from the origin. The angle between r⃗ and p⃗ is 30°. Find angular momentum magnitude.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Use the cross-product magnitude
    L = rp sin θ
  2. Substitute
    L = (2)(3) sin 30°
  3. Evaluate with SI units
    L = 6(0.5) = 3 kg m² s⁻¹

Final answer: L = 3 kg m² s⁻¹

Examiner’s tip: Angular momentum is a vector; the question asks for its non-negative magnitude.

Additional SLO practice 29 · P-11-B-02

Cross product by the determinant method

Question: Given A⃗ = î + 2ĵ + 3k̂ and B⃗ = 4î − ĵ + 2k̂, find A⃗ × B⃗.

Reveal step-by-step solution
  1. Write the cross product using components
    A⃗ × B⃗ = (AᵧB_z−A_zBᵧ)î + (A_zBₓ−AₓB_z)ĵ + (AₓBᵧ−AᵧBₓ)k̂
  2. Calculate î component
    (2)(2) − (3)(−1) = 7
  3. Calculate ĵ component
    (3)(4) − (1)(2) = 10
  4. Calculate k̂ component
    (1)(−1) − (2)(4) = −9

Final answer: A⃗ × B⃗ = 7î + 10ĵ − 9k̂

Examiner’s tip: Check the middle determinant sign carefully. The result is perpendicular to both input vectors.
