Name: _________________________________ Date: _________________

Model assumptions: constant g = 9.8 m/s²; upward is positive; neglect air resistance. Use the simulation to answer these questions.

A. Observe and predict

  1. Release a ball from 45 m. Record the time to the ground, velocity just before impact, and the shape of each motion graph.
  2. Increase mass from 1 kg to 5 kg. Does the fall time change? Explain.
  3. Double the initial drop height (e.g. 40 m to 80 m). Does time to ground double? Use a calculation.
  4. Throw a ball upward from ground level at 20 m/s. Determine time to maximum height, maximum height and total flight time.
  5. For an upward throw, explain why velocity is zero at the peak but acceleration is not.

B. Numerical practice

  1. Find the time needed to fall from 78.4 m, assuming initial speed zero.
  2. Calculate the final velocity for a body dropped from rest for 4.0 s.
  3. A ball is thrown vertically upward at 14.7 m/s. Find its highest point above the launch position.

C. Reflection

How are the slopes of height–time and velocity–time graphs related to instantaneous velocity and acceleration?
