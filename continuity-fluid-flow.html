Steady mass flow: ρ₁A₁v₁ = ρ₂A₂v₂. For an incompressible fluid of constant density: A₁v₁ = A₂v₂.

Derivation from a moving fluid slice

Step 1 — Volume moving through section 1: ΔV₁ = A₁v₁Δt.
Step 2 — Mass moved: Δm₁ = ρ₁A₁v₁Δt. Similarly, Δm₂ = ρ₂A₂v₂Δt.
Step 3 — Conservation of mass: Δm₁ = Δm₂ in steady flow without leaks.
Step 4 — For incompressible flow: ρ₁ = ρ₂, so A₁v₁ = A₂v₂.

Ideal-fluid assumptions

This demonstration uses steady flow, an incompressible fluid, a full pipe, no leaks and no modeled viscous losses. It does not calculate pressure or predict whether a real pump can maintain the specified flow.
