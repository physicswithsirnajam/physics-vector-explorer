Privacy & Account Information

This page describes how the current Physics with Sir Najam portal prototype handles accounts and saved practice records. It is a technical disclosure for pilot testing, not yet a finalized public privacy policy or legal notice.

Information collected

Firebase Authentication processes your sign-in identity, including your email address, account identifier and authentication method. The portal stores a minimal learner profile with a display name and timestamps. When you explicitly select “Save to My Physics Progress,” it stores self-practice attempt details such as question IDs, answers, outcome identifiers, score and date. Browsers may locally retain recent-question preferences.

Why it is used

To authenticate accounts, let learners revisit saved attempts, calculate SLO-based practice summaries, and display personal learning recommendations. These are self-practice records; they are not secure, verified school examination results.

Who can access it?

Current Firestore rules restrict self-practice attempts to the verified account owner. The sole administrator can read a limited directory of basic learner profiles and account-deletion requests, not the private question-by-question histories of independent learners. Users should not share PDF or JSON exports publicly.

Public content and external websites

Some linked resources remain on public GitHub Pages and Google Sites, accessible without portal login. Links to external Google services have their own privacy practices. The portal cannot yet promise that all linked learning materials are access-restricted.

Access, export and deletion requests

After signing in, open “My Physics Progress” to download a PDF report, CSV, or detailed JSON archive. The report covers only the loaded attempts; load older pages to obtain all currently saved practice attempts. In “My Privacy & Account Data,” you may create a private request for deletion review. This request does not automatically delete the Firebase Authentication account or Firestore records. Processing currently requires separate administrator identity checks and deletion work.

Retention and release status

No automatic record-expiry process is in operation in this pilot. Records remain until they are removed through a verified administrative process. A formal retention schedule, deletion process, child/student safeguards, and support contact procedures must be finalized before broad public enrollment.

Before inviting learners: the portal remains a controlled pilot. Do not enter sensitive information into practice records; do not use this app to store high-stakes grades or payment details.
