## The necessary textbook graph-axis qualification

**NBF printed Fig7.14, p122 places force/weight horizontally and extension vertically**. For a general loading path, mechanical work is `∫F dx` and is the area under the **force-vertical, extension-horizontal** plot, not automatically the area under the displayed reversed-axis nonlinear curve. In the printed graph's explicitly straight **zero-start** segment, both triangular areas have the numerical value `½Fx`. Preserve this distinction in explanations. Q36 and Q77 invoke the source's linear-loading approximation, not a claim about all force histories.
