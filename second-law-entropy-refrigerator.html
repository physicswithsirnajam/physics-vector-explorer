Mechanical energy becomes dispersed

Slide the friction control to convert useful mechanical energy into thermal energy. Watch the energy accounting and estimate entropy increase of a large thermal reservoir.

Energy is not destroyed by friction. Some organized mechanical energy is dispersed as thermal energy, making it less available for useful mechanical work. This illustrates energy degradation and irreversibility.

Idealized illustration: the large reservoir remains at approximately constant T. The entropy relation is applied only to that reservoir; this is not a full entropy calculation for every real frictional process.
