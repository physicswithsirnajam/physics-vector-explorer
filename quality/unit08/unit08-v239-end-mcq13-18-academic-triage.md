## Academic alignment and scientific interpretation

- **MCQ 13:** Candidate printed outcome **P-12-C-01** (Knowledge), exactly as captured in V224; code anomaly is outstanding. No silent renumbering to a Grade XI code.
- **MCQ 14:** Candidate **P-11-C-12** (temperature versus internal energy, Understanding), with **P-11-C-10** (constant-temperature P–V diagram, Application) as a secondary link; the ideal-gas qualification and original-figure dependence are substantial assessment concerns.
- **MCQ 15:** A state-function analogy exists in the chapter, but the recorded original official outcomes do not directly certify the multiple-choice formulation. **Do not force** an unverified SLO. Content ambiguity itself requires repair/exclusion.
- **MCQ 16:** Direct candidate **P-11-C-04** (Boltzmann constant as R/\(N_A\), Knowledge). Check the source notation before digital rendering; p.130 uses \(N_A\).
- **MCQs 17–18:** **No direct mapped summative outcome in V224's Domain C** for expansion of solid materials. The underlying physics is not thereby incorrect, nor does it prove the topic is absent from every other curriculum domain; an official mapping must be independently authenticated before assigning graded use.

The figure in MCQ 14 is an isothermal-looking curve labelled **T = Constant**. A plotted or pictured (P)–(V) path alone does **not** determine internal-energy change for an arbitrary material; ideal-gas model assumptions matter. It is **not permissible to drop the figure** in online MCQ import.

The thermal-expansion question **17 spans printed pp.151–152**, and its last option is part of the original question. It must not be shortened, guessed, or rewritten as a one-page multiple-choice import.
