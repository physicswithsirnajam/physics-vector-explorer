## Internal physics validation principles (not student answer keys)

- For a Carnot heat engine, use absolute reservoir temperatures and distinguish its ideal maximum efficiency from the actual efficiency of an arbitrary engine. A physical heat input is not interchangeable with net work output.
- For reversed Carnot refrigeration, cooling COP has **cold-side heat** in its numerator and requires **the cold and hot absolute reservoir temperatures**. The actual COP of a non-ideal refrigerator is not enough to solve for one temperature.
- When comparing efficiencies after a temperature change, clearly identify what is held constant. “Double 200 °C” is **not** the same statement as doubling an absolute temperature in kelvin.
- Process work is **path-dependent**, so comparisons require a defined basis; “maximum” as an unqualified claim is not an answerable question.
- A system-boundary description cannot answer a question about the definition of an entire branch of physics. Do not manufacture an answer simply because four choices are printed.
