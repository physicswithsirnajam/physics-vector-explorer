Experiment 3 · Beats from slightly different frequencies

When two waves with close but unequal frequencies overlap, their resultant varies from strong to weak and back. These are beats, not a fixed coherent interference pattern.

For two equal-amplitude sine waves, envelope amplitude = 2A |cos(π(f₂ − f₁)t)|. Equal frequencies yield a constant envelope, not repeating beats.
Musical instrument connection: Musicians can tune two notes by listening for slow beats, then adjust one frequency until the beating disappears.
