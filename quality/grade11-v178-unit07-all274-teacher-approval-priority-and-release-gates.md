## 3. Safe teacher decision workflow (planned, not deployed)

Use a **source section + original question number** as the primary identity, plus the previously recorded original row or image-region SHA. Do not identify only "Q7.1" because the textbook short, comprehensive and numerical sections each reuse numbering.

Suggested per-item private workflow:

1. Open the owner-original item and its exact page/Word row; confirm source hash or scan location without rewriting the original.
2. Independently validate **physical assumptions, numeric units, answer key and explanation**; for MCQs, one defensibly correct choice plus three scientifically credible distinct distractors.
3. Flag whether the original itself must be preserved and a **separate corrected candidate** created; do not overwrite the keyed source Word or NBF PDF.
4. Check stimulus completeness, mobile/projector accessibility, and source-permission/licensing, especially for apparatus/graph diagrams.
5. Check the **exact official summative SLO**, its Knowledge/Understanding/Application demand and how the question actually asks students to reason.
6. Obtain **authenticated individual teacher science, cognition and scoring signoffs** on the precise corrected or original candidate version, with an auditable time and responsible reviewer in the protected backend—not in a public GitHub JSON.
7. Only consider graded student release **after** the V160/V161 teacher backend staging/production NO-GO and App Check reporting requirements are explicitly resolved. **Green code CI is not a teacher credential or authorization.**

**Batching:** Begin with **at most 20 question identities per review batch**, using urgent source blockers first and retaining topic diversity. V178 does not claim completion of teacher review merely because it creates the queue. An ordinary source item is not “pre-approved”; every one of the 274 must eventually be checked for its intended use.
