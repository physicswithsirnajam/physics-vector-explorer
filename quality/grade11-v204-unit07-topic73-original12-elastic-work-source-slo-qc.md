## Critical NBF Fig.7.14 axis limitation

The **actual printed p122 Figure 7.14** places weight/force on the **horizontal** axis and extension on the **vertical** axis. General work is `W=∫F dx` in a conventional graph with extension horizontal and force vertical; the geometric area under the **reversed-axis** curve is `∫x dF`, not necessarily work. But in the book's explicitly drawn **linear, zero-origin Hookean segment**, each triangular area is `½Fx`, so the source's stated numerical triangle result for that segment is correct. Do not claim a general nonlinear reversed-axis area represents physical work. The figure must be explained accurately rather than silently corrected or misrepresented as a different source diagram.
