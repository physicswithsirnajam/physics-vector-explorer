## Source integrity and physics QA to preserve

In NBF's proportional region, **`F=kx`**, **`U = ½kx²`**, **`k = YA/L`**, **`ΔL = FL/(YA)`**. The area under a general loading curve **`∫F dx`** remains the correct mechanical work, including nonlinear loading. For a *perfectly reversible* elastic process, that work may be recovered during unloading; beyond the elastic limit, **irreversibility can prevent the loading work from equalling recoverable stored elastic energy**.

**Independent counterexample for Q35:** take a final extension X and final force F. For a linear wire `F₁(s)=Fs/X`, the work is `FX/2`. For an elastic but nonlinear wire `F₂(s)=F(s/X)²`, the work is `FX/3`. Both terminate at `(X,F)`, yet their energies differ. The future scored stem must specify the loading shape, not merely the endpoint.

A further distinction matters for Q31–33: an originally assigned **P-11-B-25** outcome can be source-aligned to an experiment, but the official SLO demands **Understanding**, whereas source-authored Application questions about wire geometry, scaling and Young's modulus may merit **P-11-B-24** when scored. The final teacher decides alignment based on the *actual question demand*, not a blanket rule that anything containing Y is B-24. The original 80-question DOCX has not been reclassified.
