Teacher notes • classroom and home

Projector discussion: Ask students to predict the resultant before clicking one of the presets. Switch between head-to-tail and parallelogram to demonstrate that both methods produce the same resultant.

  • Double the magnitudes of both vectors. What happens to |R|?
  • Rotate B until the resultant is smallest. Why?
  • Make a right triangle using 3 N and 4 N. Explain the 5 N resultant.

Curriculum alignment: P-11-B-01 (resolving 2D vectors into perpendicular components) is a summative outcome. Vector addition is presented here as a supporting conceptual extension; consult the textbook and current framework before assigning examination weight.

Physics with Sir Najam — Vector Addition Investigation

Grade XI · Unit 2 · Name: ________________________ Date: ______________

Aim: Investigate how two vectors combine using the component, head-to-tail and parallelogram methods.

Experiment 1 — Observe and calculate

  1. Set A = 6 N at 30°, and B = 4 N at 120°. Record Aₓ, Aᵧ, Bₓ and Bᵧ.
  2. Calculate Rₓ, Rᵧ, magnitude of R and its angle. Compare with the app.
  3. Change to the parallelogram method. Does the resultant change? Explain.

Experiment 2 — Predict before testing

  1. Choose A = 3 N right and B = 4 N up. Predict the resultant magnitude and direction.
  2. Make the vectors equal and opposite. Explain the resultant and what happens to its direction.
  3. What common mistake occurs when students add only the two magnitudes?

Reflection: Explain in one sentence why vector direction is essential in addition.
