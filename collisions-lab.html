4. Work out the physics

Calculate before you reveal the final velocities.

pbefore = m₁u₁ + m₂u₂
pafter = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂

KE = ½mv²

For a 1D elastic collision:

v₁ = [(m₁ − m₂)u₁ + 2m₂u₂] / (m₁ + m₂)

v₂ = [2m₁u₁ + (m₂ − m₁)u₂] / (m₁ + m₂)

When the objects stick together:

v = (m₁u₁ + m₂u₂) / (m₁ + m₂)

6. Teacher and student activities

Use this five-part investigation in class or for independent practice.

1 · Predict: What happens when two equal masses collide elastically and the second is initially stationary?

2 · Test: Run the equal-mass preset and compare before/after values.

3 · Change: Switch to a perfectly inelastic collision without changing the masses or velocities.

4 · Explain: Why does total momentum remain constant while kinetic energy can decrease?

5 · Extend: Try the textbook Example 3.7 and explain any negative final velocities.

Classroom ready
Curriculum scope: The collision analysis supports summative SLOs P-11-B-12 and P-11-B-13. General momentum-conservation examples also support formative P-11-B-11. No coefficient of restitution is needed.
