2. How does changing rpm affect the acceleration requirement?

Doubling rpm quadruples ac at fixed radius; doubling radius doubles ac at fixed rpm. Neither relation directly predicts the separation time of a sample.

Think like an FBISE student

To justify separation, describe the spinning tube, the inward force required to follow a circular path, the different responses of components, and the collection of denser suspended material toward the outer tube end. Do not confuse the rotating-frame apparent centrifugal effect with an independent applied force in the laboratory frame.
