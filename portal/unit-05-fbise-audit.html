Unit 5: FBISE SLO & Assessment Audit

The supplied official FBISE HSSC-I Physics Assessment Framework directly confirms the theory-examination classification and cognitive domain of all four Work and Energy SLOs. This page explains exactly what is verified—and what still requires academic review.

2. What the FBISE HSSC-I model paper actually requires

The source's full Grade XI theory paper totals 80 marks, split equally between objective and descriptive sections. This structure applies to the whole syllabus; it is not an 80-MCQ quota for a single Unit 5 test.

Section A — 40 marks

80 MCQs × 0.5 mark, made up of 56 independent MCQs and 24 contextual MCQs spanning six scenario/stimulus groups.

Section B — 28 marks

Seven short-response questions, each worth 4 marks, with internal OR choice.

Section C — 12 marks

Two extended-response questions, each worth 6 marks, with internal OR choice.

FBISE question format

The model shows MCQs in a boxed four-option A/B/C/D table with answer bubbles. Preserve this familiar format in future practice papers.

Source: official framework PDF pp.28 and 30. The model's sample questions and SLO alignment pages (42–43) include B-31, B-32 and B-30, confirming that these outcomes occur within the full-grade paper. Sample item-level cognitive tags need not replace the parent SLO's official cognitive classification.

3. Practical Based Assessment: a separate determination

Do not infer a practical/PBA exemption

The supplied framework explicitly notes on PDF page 27 that it does not identify summative SLOs for Practical Based Assessment (PBA) and that a separate list of practicals will be provided. Therefore:

  • We can confirm the four theory SLO classifications using PDF p.11.
  • We cannot conclude from this document alone that Unit 5 has no assessed practical activities.
  • A separate official PBA list or model is necessary before making a formal practical-assessment coverage claim.

5. Gates before a Unit 5 assessment release

  1. Keep formal written theory items within B-29–B-32 and verify the targeted cognitive level for each question; do not exclude legitimate conceptual teaching from the classroom.
  2. Independently solve every proposed question, numerical, keyed option and distractor. Flag and correct source errors instead of copying them into assessments.
  3. Check the original NBF chapter and printed page against each item. Do not introduce adjacent units or invent new summative SLO classifications.
  4. Retain the original FBISE four-option boxed MCQ table and bubble layout for practice sheets; distinguish full-grade paper quotas from an appropriately sized single-unit test.
  5. Keep students' questions separated from any teacher answer/marking resource; no public reveal during timed student tests.
  6. Obtain a teacher sign-off for academic accuracy and printed/mobile/projector quality, and check the separately published PBA list for any practical coverage.
