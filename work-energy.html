Question 1 / 6

Derive kinetic energy — guided steps (P-11-B-29)

  1. Begin with Newton's second law: F = ma.
  2. For constant acceleration in a straight line: vf² − vi² = 2ad.
  3. Rearrange: a = (vf² − vi²)/(2d).
  4. Use W = Fd = mad (net force and displacement parallel).
  5. Substitute to get Wnet = ½mvf² − ½mvi² = ΔK.

Assumes a constant net force and straight-line displacement; the general work–energy theorem extends to variable forces via integration.
