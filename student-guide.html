Important access information

What to know before practising

Can I use the notes and Test Builder without signing in?

Yes. The current public study pages, simulations, Test Builder and MCQ practice can be opened without signing in. Personalized Learning Hub screens and private account progress require signing in and, where applicable, enrollment. Public learning resources are not made private simply by appearing in the signed-in dashboard.

Can I save my combined-unit test result to My Physics Progress?

Not yet. Personal cloud saving currently supports single-unit tests from Unit 1 or Unit 2 only, for eligible signed-in, verified accounts. Combined-unit tests and individual Unit 3–5 tests provide an on-screen report that you can print or save as a PDF. Do not treat the scores as verified examination grades.

What if Start MCQ Practice does not appear?

Check that you selected at least one published unit and MCQs only. Your requested number of questions must be available under your SLO, difficulty and other filters. For combined practice, every selected unit must contribute at least one eligible question. Complete scenario-only selections must have enough full source scenario groups. Broaden the filters or reduce the requested question count.

Are these official FBISE papers and examiner-approved questions?

No. These are teacher-prepared, source-based provisional self-practice resources intended to support understanding and revision. The available source mappings and automated checks do not replace full independent academic verification or official FBISE examination rules.
