Amplitude here means wave displacement amplitude (not detector area). The amplitude-ratio squared controls the relative intensity.
NBF Assignment 9.1: To increase intensity by a factor of 50, amplitude must increase by √50 ≈ 7.07.

Extension · Energy spreading from an isotropic point source

Additional model beyond the two equations explicitly developed on NBF pp. 155–156; provided for conceptual enrichment.

Expanding spherical wavefronts

In an ideal, non-absorbing medium, a source radiating equally in all directions spreads its power over the area of a sphere (4πr²).

Model limitations: This inverse-square rule assumes an isotropic point source, no absorption, and the far field. It is not a universal rule for every wave geometry (for example, an ideal plane wave).
